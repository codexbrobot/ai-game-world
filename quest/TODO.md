# Fix list

Small things to fix later.

- [ ] **The pit by the Warden's door traps you.** In the Brine Gaol, the chasm at row 10, columns 8–9 (`x` in the Gaol's `heights` in `js/data.js`) has no way out, so falling in can end a run that should go on. Put a ladder (or a climbable wall) in it so you can crawl out. Standing in it could cost 1 HP every 3 seconds or so, but it must never stop you finishing the game.
- [x] **Footsteps in free movement are too loud and too ticky.** In step movement you hear one per square, but walking freely fires one roughly every world unit (`stepped()` in `src/engine.js`, from the head-bob phase), so it becomes a fast, loud tick-tick-tick. The sound itself (`FX.step` in `js/sound.js`, out at gain 1.7) is a sharp noise click plus a thump. Redo it: much quieter, softer and duller (a scuff on stone, a little different each time), fewer of them when walking freely, and maybe a recorded set of footsteps (stone, wet stone, wading, cobbles in the rain) like the monsters have now.
- [x] **Take the Water (test) choice out of Settings.** The test is done; the Gaol is flooded, and players shouldn't be able to drain it. Remove the Flooded/Dry row from `showSettings()` in `js/game.js` and its handler, always run with water (`Q.prefs.flood` true, `world.setWater(true)`), and ignore any old `quest-borg-flood` = `dry` already saved in someone's browser. Drop the "water" mention from the Settings row of the controls table in `README.md`.
- [ ] **Finding things should get a DM box, not just a coin sound.** Searching remains (✚) in the Gaol and elsewhere only plays the pickup sound and writes a line in the log: silver, a poultice, an Omen, a scroll under the bones (`searchRemains()` in `js/game.js`). Only strange items get a box (`offerItem`). Give every find a DM box saying what you found and, if it's known, what it does (a scroll's effect from `Q.SCROLLS`, what a poultice heals, what an Omen is for). The narrator will need these lines recorded too (the silver amount spoken as "some silver", like the toll).
- [x] **The guard room's guards attack before the DM sets the scene.** In the Gaol's guard room (the cards on the table; `guardroom` in `js/story.js`, area 5,5 to 9,7) the drowned guards come at you as soon as the door opens, so by the time you walk in and get "The guard room … They look up." they're usually already dead. Have them sitting at the table, not moving or noticing you, until you're properly in the room and the DM's box has been shown; then "Raise the femur" starts the fight.

## Bigger ideas

- [ ] **Start every new game with the same two pre-made wretches in the Gaol.** It's a roguelite and wretches die a lot, but the first run should get people out of the prison without much hassle, give them a feel for the game, and let them beat some people up. Two fixed characters would also be a better way into the story; once the larger story is under way, it matters less who carries it on. After them, new wretches are rolled or made from the book as now (the heirs who wake at a class home in Skarnvik, `startCreate` / `acceptParty` in `js/game.js`). Still to decide:
  - who they are: names, classes, a line of who they were and why they were in the Brine;
  - their abilities, HP and gear, tuned so the Gaol is winnable but still dangerous;
  - decent weapons waiting for them when they get into the storage area (the evidence room, where `restoreGear` gives your things back);
  - what the title screen offers: a new game always starts with the two, and rolling or making your own is for the heirs;
  - with fixed names, the narrator could say them; the lines that say "one of you" could be recorded again with their names for the Gaol.
