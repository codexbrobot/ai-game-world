## First: an inventory

- [ ] **Items you can carry, not just wear.** Today each wretch has exactly one weapon, one armor and one relic, worn, and nothing else. Anything new replaces what you had: in the evidence room both wretches put on their warhammers, then the uncollected sack turned up the Widow's Blade, and giving it to one of them *dropped* the warhammer for good. No way to try it, change your mind, go back to the hammer, or sell the spare at Thrumm's. (The same gap is behind the dead prisoner's boots.) Build an inventory that works the way MÖRK BORG does:
  - **The book's rule, to check against the book:** a wretch can carry Strength + 8 normal-sized things without trouble; up to twice that and they're *encumbered* (DR +2 on Strength and Agility tests); more than that they can't carry at all. Small things (coins, a ring, a tooth) don't count. A container (a sack, a backpack) can carry more, as in the book's starting gear. The rules and mechanics are free to use under the licence; only the book's text and art aren't.
  - **What each wretch has:** the weapon in hand, the armor worn, the relic worn (cursed ones still won't come off), and a pack of everything else: spare weapons, armor, relics, found things (boots, a ring, the d66 finds), maybe scrolls and poultices (decide: keep those shared by the party, as now, or carried by one wretch each, as the book has it).
  - **Doing things with it:** wield or wear from the pack (any time outside a fight; in a fight, drawing another weapon costs your turn), give to the other wretch, drop, and sell at Thrumm's. New things go into the pack by default, with "wield it now" as a choice, so nothing is lost.
  - **Where you see it:** the character sheet (tap a portrait) gets the pack, with how much they carry out of Strength + 8 and whether they're encumbered; Party shows both. Encumbered shows on the HUD chip too.
  - **Hooks:** the evidence room's things go on, and the spare into the pack; `offerItem` (strange things) asks who carries it, not who swaps; the dead's packs are lost with them (or left where they fell, for the next pair to find?); saves from before keep working (an empty pack).
  - Spec it: picking up, swapping back, giving, selling, encumbrance, the evidence room and the Widow's Blade case.
