    The Vorn Graveyard

    Wretch

    The world is dying. You are nobody.

    The comet has burned for nine nights. In the drowned town of Skarnvik the gates will be chained at the next dark moon, and nobody left inside will see another spring.

    A stranger with a sewn-shut eye found you in the gutter and pressed a copper into your palm. Beneath the Vorn mausoleum lies the Reliquary of the Hollow Saint. Bring it to me and you will have a purse of gold and a seat on the last cart out.

    The graveyard is not empty. It never is.
